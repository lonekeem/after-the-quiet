# After the Quiet — Series Bible
*Lone Keem Studios · working title · original IP*

## Premise
Lone Keem, Maine is a small harbor town of 4,000 people, pine hills, one bridge, one diner, and a paper mill that closed years ago. In late October, a sickness called "the Quiet" (because the infected stop speaking first) spreads from the coast. In a single night the town falls.

The story is told by **Nia Hart, grown up**, looking back on the night her big brother Kemoy carried her out of Lone Keem. Her warm, gentle, unhurried voice frames each chapter like a bedtime story. We play as 13-year-old Kemoy; Nia was four, and tells his story as he told it to her.

Tone: grim, frightening, grounded. Moments of tenderness are the point. The violence is there to show what is lost, not as spectacle.

## Chapter 1 — "The Day Before" (≈15–25 min)
**Cold open (v1.2):** a 60–90s skippable trailer: burning city, jammed highway, burning suburb, horde on a bridge, flooded street, dead mall, subway, snowy Maine town. Grown-up Nia narrates (on-screen text) over a deep drone score, then a hard cut to black: "The day before..."

**Prologue (title card, narration):** "Everybody remembers where they were the day the world ended. I remember the day before. It was the best day my brother ever had, in Lone Keem, Maine."

**Act I — An Ordinary Saturday (warm)**
1. Kemoy wakes in his bedroom. Morning light, fog over the harbor. Objective: get dressed / grab sketchbook.
2. Kitchen: Dad burns pancakes, Mom comes in from a night shift, Nia demands the rabbit gets a pancake too. Radio plays a cheerful local station, WLKM, "Lone Keem Morning". Interact with family.
3. Yard: Theo comes over; they toss a football. Objective: find the ball in the hedge.
4. Walt's porch: Walt gives Kemoy his old brass compass. "So you can always find your way home." He coughs. Mentions a "bad flu down the coast."
5. Main Street: walk to the Lighthouse Diner with Dad. Ruth gives Nia a free milkshake. Town sign: "Welcome to Lone Keem, Maine. Est. 1791."

**Act II — Creeping Hints**
5b. Afternoon (v1.2): comics on Theo's porch steps, fishing with Dad on the town dock ("Every Saturday. That is a promise."), piggyback ride for Nia along the harbor path at sunset.
6. Radio at the diner: "…reports of unrest in Portland… residents advised to stay indoors…" Ruth turns it off.
7. Far-off sirens. A helicopter. Dad's phone keeps buzzing. Mom is called in to the hospital early. Goodbye hug at the door (important).
8. Evening: Walt doesn't answer his door; his tomatoes are trampled. Kemoy sees him standing very still in his yard, facing the wall.
9. Dinner without Mom. Power flickers. Nia: "Is Mom okay?" Kemoy draws her a comic to make her laugh.

**Act III — The Night It Changes (horror)**
10. 2 AM. Glass breaks. Dad goes to check. The radio repeats an emergency broadcast. Objective: find the flashlight.
11. First encounter: Walt is in the kitchen. He is not Walt anymore. Dad fights him off. Escape through the garage.
12. Street: fog, fires, car alarms. Infected shamble through. Objective: reach Theo's house. Stealth/escape with flashlight and a baseball bat (simple combat: shove/strike).
13. Theo's house: Theo's family is gone; Theo joins.
14. Run to Hart's Auto to get the truck. Short horde chase.
15. **Heartbreak:** At the garage, Mom's car pulls in, crashed. Dana got out of the hospital to find them, but she's been bitten. She tells Kemoy he is braver than he knows, gives Nia's rabbit back to her, and asks Marcus to go. She closes the garage door herself.

**Act IV — Cliffhanger**
16. Drive/run to the Harbor Bridge, which has sandbags and army lights. Dad stays back to hold the gate as the horde arrives. "Get your sister across. I'll be right behind you."
17. Across the bridge, the lights die. The radio crackles: a voice says Kemoy's name. Black screen. Grown-up Nia, by the fire with Biscuit: "Dad said he would be right behind us. Kemoy and I told ourselves that, for a long, long time."
**END OF CHAPTER 1**

## Future Chapters (notes)
- **Ch 2 — "High Water":** Flooded lower Lone Keem after the dam breaks. Wading streets with hordes, rooftops. Finding the source of the radio voice.
- **Ch 3 — "The Bridge":** Military checkpoint on the sandbagged bridge; a soldier, Sgt. Imani Cole, becomes an ally. A choice: who gets through.
- **Ch 4 — "Atrium":** Survivors holding out in Pinewood Mall. Community, a fragile peace, betrayal. Then the subway tunnels under Bangor.
- **Ch 5 — "First Snow":** Snowy small town up north. Kemoy finds Marcus, or what's left of the truth. Walt's compass points home.
