# After the Quiet — Story Mode (v1.3.0) · Mature 18+
Lone Keem Studios presents. Riverside, Maine. One night. Text-only dialogue.

**Cast:** Keem (lead, a Black boy with a backpack) · Dad (Marcus) · Nia (little sister) and Biscuit the rabbit · Mr. Walt and Mr. Dube (neighbors) · Ruth (deputy) · Big Gus (the barber, mini-boss).

0. **The House** — Screams wake Keem in his boarded-up house. Through the gaps in the boards he watches Mr. Dube tackled and Mr. Walt being eaten. Gear up: backpack, bat, Dad's lockbox pistol (with a very Dad note). Pry the door.
1. **The Street** — Fight through the yard and the first runners toward the Pell house.
2. **The Pell House** — Dad, Nia and Biscuit. Banter. Get the patrol rifle from Ruth's cruiser while she is being eaten.
3. **Hank's Garage** — A horde. Shoot the fuel tanker. Big boom.
4. **The Truck** — Drive, ram zombies, crash near the bridge.
5. **Big Gus** — Mini-boss: ground slams and charges. 900 HP.
6. **The Bridge** — The family crosses. Dad stays behind to hold the gate: "Right behind you."

Collectibles: 8 *Captain Riverside* comic pages. Death = bite/feast animation, red YOU DIED, retry from the chapter checkpoint.

## Chapter 2 · High Water (v1.5.0)
Minutes after the bridge. Keem and Nia reach Haverford, the next town south. The radio calls it a "safe zone." It isn't one.
- **The Checkpoint:** the National Guard post is overrun, and the soldiers are being eaten. In the command tent, a dying Sgt. Okafor's radio message says the last evac helicopter leaves Haverford High stadium at 0600. The gate runs on a backup panel that needs fuses. Grabbing the Guard's supplies unlocks the **Guard SAW** in the Armory.
- **High Water (free roam):** the town is flooded. Find 3 fuses at Haverford Hardware, the Doughnut Depot, and the Suds-n-Duds laundromat (spin-cycle massacre included). There is loot to grab and Captain Riverside pages #9–#14 to find.
- **Hold the Gate (horde):** survive 60 seconds of waves while Nia wires the panel. Dad shows up, "right behind you," two hours late with a doughnut, and starts blasting.
- **Boss: Coach Brickman:** the gym teacher who failed Keem, now a 1.75× linebacker zombie.
- **Cliffhanger:** the scoreboard says the evac left early and to go to Portland. Dad's sleeve is wet with blood. "It's nothing, baby. Glass. From the gate."
New voices (Kokoro stock): Sgt. Okafor (am_eric, radio filter) and Coach Brickman (am_onyx).


## Chapter 3 — Route 1
On the road to Portland with a near-empty tank. The family clears the Big Moose Truck Stop for diesel, and at the Pine Tree Motel Keem finds the bite on Dad's arm from the Haverford gate. Dad: "Don't tell your sister. Not yet." Boss: Big Rig Ronnie. New weapon: chainsaw. The Tollmen announce over the radio that they own the turnpike.

## Chapter 4 — Everybody Pays
At the Maine Turnpike toll plaza the Tollmen raiders (human enemies that strafe and shoot) ambush the family. Deacon takes Dad and Nia because "the Captain pays double for biters." Keem storms the camp, breaks the cage and kills Deacon. New weapon: lever-action .30-30. Dad loses feeling in his hand.

## Chapter 5 — Black Friday
The Maine Mall. New survivors: Dr. Imani Reyes, a veterinarian, and Tuck, a kiosk kid. Keem finds three antiviral packs while the others hold the doors so Reyes can dose Dad; it buys him days. Boss: Mall Santa. New weapon: Auto-12 shotgun. Reyes has heard a "Navy" broadcast about a cure trial at Fort Williams that wants infected volunteers.

## Chapter 6 — The Lighthouse
Fort Williams and Portland Head Light. The family survives a Tollmen raid and kills the Toll King, whose last words are "The Captain… he doesn't cure them…". They signal the ship, and the "Captain" radios back: bring the infected one to the shore, alone. Dad's eyes go gray: "Keem… run." One gunshot, off-screen. **To be continued — Chapter 7 coming soon.**
