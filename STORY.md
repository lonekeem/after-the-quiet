# After the Quiet — Story Mode (v1.3.0) · Mature 18+
Lone Keem Studios presents. Riverside, Maine. One night. Text-only dialogue.

**Cast:** Keem (lead, a Black boy with a backpack) · Dad (Marcus) · Nia (little sister) and Biscuit the rabbit · Mr. Walt and Mr. Dube (neighbors) · Ruth (deputy) · Big Gus (the barber, mini-boss).

0. **The House** — Screams wake Keem in his boarded-up house. Through the gaps in the boards he watches Mr. Dube tackled and Mr. Walt being eaten. Gear up: backpack, bat, Dad's lockbox pistol (with a very Dad note). Pry the door.
1. **The Street** — Fight through the yard and the first runners toward the Pell house.
2. **The Pell House** — Dad, Nia and Biscuit. Banter. Get the patrol rifle from Ruth's cruiser while she is being eaten.
3. **Hank's Garage** — A horde. Shoot the fuel tanker. Big boom.
4. **The Truck** — Drive, ram zombies, crash near the bridge.
5. **Big Gus** — Mini-boss: ground slams and charges. 900 HP.
6. **The Bridge** — The family crosses. Dad stays behind to hold the gate: "Right behind you."

Collectibles: 8 *Captain Riverside* comic pages. Death = bite/feast animation, red YOU DIED, retry from the chapter checkpoint.
